## Overview

*This software is neither affiliated with nor endorsed by either Control4 or
Schluter-Systems.*

Control Schluter DITRA-HEAT-E-WiFi floor-heating thermostats from Control4. Each
thermostat is a native Control4 thermostat with its floor temperature, setpoint,
mode and full weekly heating schedule, so heated floors sit beside the rest of
the home's climate control. Changes made at the thermostat or in the Schluter
app show in Control4 as they happen.

## Features

- A native Control4 thermostat for each Schluter thermostat
- Floor temperature, heat setpoint and Heat or Off mode, in the project's
  temperature scale
- The weekly heating schedule, read and edited from the standard Control4
  scheduling screen
- Until Next and Permanent hold
- Live updates when the thermostat or the Schluter app makes a change
- Every thermostat on the account found, ready to bind to a Schluter Thermostat
  driver
- Floor temperature as a connection for other drivers

## Included drivers

### Schluter

The account driver. Sign in once with the Schluter DITRA-HEAT WiFi account, and
it finds every thermostat on the account and holds one connection for all of
them.

### Schluter Thermostat

A native Control4 thermostat for one Schluter thermostat, with floor
temperature, setpoint, mode, hold and the weekly schedule. Add one for each
thermostat and bind it to the account driver.

## System requirements

- Control4 OS 3.3+
- A Schluter DITRA-HEAT-E-WiFi account with each thermostat set up in the
  Schluter DITRA-HEAT WiFi app
- Internet access from the Control4 controller

## Compatibility

Works with the Schluter DITRA-HEAT-E-RS1 and DITRA-HEAT-E-RT1 thermostats.
