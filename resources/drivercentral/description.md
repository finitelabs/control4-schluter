## Overview

Control Schluter DITRA-HEAT-E-WiFi floor-heating thermostats from Control4. Each
thermostat is a native Control4 thermostat with floor temperature, setpoint,
mode and its weekly heating schedule, through the homeowner's Schluter
DITRA-HEAT WiFi account.

## Features

- A native Control4 thermostat for each Schluter thermostat
- Floor temperature, heat setpoint and Heat or Off mode
- The weekly heating schedule, read and edited from the standard Control4
  scheduling screen
- Until Next and Permanent hold
- Every thermostat on the account found, ready to bind to a Schluter Thermostat
  driver
- Floor temperature as a connection for other drivers
- Follows the project's temperature scale

## System requirements

- Control4 OS 3.3+
- A Schluter DITRA-HEAT-E-WiFi account with a thermostat set up in the Schluter
  DITRA-HEAT WiFi app
- Internet access from the Control4 controller
